# Cleary Clubhouse

A copy of the Cleary pitching app (Cleary-PDP-s) for trying out changes.

- Opens on a clubhouse screen: tap the spots in the room to get around the app. Backgrounds were made in Google Flow with the Cleary logos added on top: `clubhouse.jpg` (wide screens) and `clubhouse-phone.jpg` (phones held upright).
- The preloaded fall 2026 pitcher profiles and Rapsodo numbers are removed (only "Test Pitcher" is left).
- Not connected to the Cleary database, so it runs as a local preview. To give it accounts, paste a Supabase URL and key at the top of `index Cleary.html`.
